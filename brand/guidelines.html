01 · The mark

One coin, both sides of the trade, and a line that won't be capped.

The mark is a single coin cut by a call payoff. Each part is something Optara actually does, so the logo explains the product.

Puts

The strike

The coin

One account, one margin. Calls (Monad Purple, above) and puts (Monad Berry, below) live in the same coin because portfolio margin lets them offset. The round counter nods to Monad's own coin-like mark.

The payoff

max(S − K, 0), the standard European call: flat below the strike, then rising one-for-one. It cuts clean through the coin.

The breakout

The ray leaves the circle and turns mint as it moves into the money. Optara options are uncapped: nothing stops the line.

The strike

An ember point where the payoff turns: the moment a trade happens. Its spark borrows the energy of Kuru, where Optara's options trade.

03 · Colour

Built on Monad's palette, lit by the trade

Monad's published colours are the foundation, so Optara looks at home in the Monad ecosystem. Three Optara colours add meaning: mint for calls and the uncapped breakout, ember for the strike (a nod to Kuru's bold energy), lavender for highlights.

05 · Usage

Do and don't

Don't rotateThe flat leg must stay level
Don't recolourNever a red ray: red means loss
Don't stretchScale proportionally
Don't lose contrastUse the white version on purple

Minimum size: 16 px for the mark, 96 px wide for the horizontal logo. Write the name lowercase in the logo and "Optara" in running text.

Applications
