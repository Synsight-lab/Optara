As a Writer — “the House”

Sell tickets for income

  1. Lock 5 WMON for 5 calls
  2. Sell 5 tickets for $2.00
  3. Wait until Friday
If ≤ $10 ✅ — worthless. You keep 5 WMON + $2.00 → profit $2.00
If $12 ⚠️ — buyer gets 0.20 WMON/ticket (1 WMON). You keep 4 WMON + $2.00

Writer wins small and often — one big move against you eats it.

As a Buyer — “the Bettor”

Buy tickets for upside

  1. Pay $2.00 (5 × $0.40)
  2. Hold or sell early on Kuru
  3. Redeem after settlement
If $12 ✅ — 0.20 WMON/ticket ≈ $1.99. 5 tickets net ≈ $9.93 → +$7.93 (4.9×)
If ≤ $10 ❌ — $0, lose $2. Max loss = premium.

Buyer wins big, less often — and you can sell early if price moves your way before Friday.

Risks — read this

You might not sell early — needs a buyer. If none, hold until Friday. Settlement still works.
Settlement isn’t instant — waits for Chainlink’s first update after expiry (≤ ~1h). Price unchanged.
Premium ≠ fair value — willing price, can be thin or manipulated. Don’t trust last price alone.
Not audited. 286 tests pass, no independent audit yet. Don’t risk more than you can lose.

FAQ

Which makes more — buying or selling? ›
Selling = small frequent wins. Buying = rare, big wins. Many do both at different times.
Can I lose more than I put in? ›
Buyer: Never — max = premium. Writer: Max = collateral − premium (no liquidation beyond).
Must I wait until Friday? ›
No — sell on Kuru anytime before expiry. After expiry, wait for settlement then redeem/claim.
Sell half?
Yes — ERC-20s. Any nonzero amount, no minimum after minting.
Where is my money?
In the vault contract — not with Kuru or a person. Un-upgradeable, un-pausable for payouts.
Chainlink down?
If Chainlink never posts after expiry, series can’t settle (V1). Funds stuck — disclosed. Only major direct feeds, ≤30d expiries.
